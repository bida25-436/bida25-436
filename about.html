Hi, Welcome To Tumelo's Crochet Creations!

My name is Tumelo Mesotlo the CEO of Tumelo's Crochet Creations (TCC) and I am very passionate about crocheting. I am an 18 year old girl originating from the village of Moshupa in the southern district of Botswana who found peace in yarn at the age of 15.

Crocheting has always been more than just a hobby to me, it is a way to express my creativity. I began learning how to crochet just from watching YouTube videos during the period of waiting for my grade 10 results in 2022 and overtime it grew into a skill when it all just started as a joke because I was anxious about my results and couldn't stay idle so I had to keep myself busy. After I saw how much potential I have, I started a business in 2024 and it has been the best thing I ever did for myself. Through this portfolio, I aim to showcase my journey and connect with people who appreciate the art of handmade crafts.

I can crochet without looking-handy for movie nights.
