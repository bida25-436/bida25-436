Hi, Welcome To Tumelo's Crochet Creations!

My name is Tumelo the CEO of Tumelo's Crochet Creations (TCC) and I am very passionate about crocheting. I am an 18 year old girl originating from the village of Moshupa who found peace in yarn at the age of 15.

Crocheting has always been more than just a hobby to me, it is a way to express my creativity. I began learning how to crochet just from watching YouTube videos during the period of waiting for my grade 10 results in 2022 and overtime it grew into a skill when it all started as a joke because I was anxious my results and couldn't stay idle so I had to keep myself busy. After I saw how much potential I have, I started a business in 2024 and it has been the best thing I ever did for myself. Through this portfolio, I aim to showcase my journey and connect with people who appreciate the art of handmade crafts.

🧶I can crochet for 3 hours straight without stopping unless it has to be a bathroom break!
